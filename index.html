Questions

The objections, answered in order.

Is this legal in Ontario?+

Yes. This is information about publicly offered prices. We do not take bets, hold funds, or link you to a sportsbook, and we are not affiliated with any operator. You must be 19 or older to bet in Ontario.

Do you guarantee a profit?+

No. Every figure is expected value, and variance on any single bet is far larger than any edge shown. We have no graded closing-line results yet; the scoreboard above fills in as games settle, and is published whichever way it goes.

Why refuse 95% of what you find?+

Because the most attractive-looking numbers are disproportionately errors: a line matched to the wrong market, a consensus resting on one other engine, a price no sharp book stands behind. The refused rows average +3.26% against +2.85% for the rows that pass. That gap is the reason the gate exists.

What if the price has moved by the time I get there?+

It may have. Every published price was re-checked at the book’s own API moments before publishing, and each row says how long ago. Prices can move in minutes, so check the number at the book before you bet. If it has moved, the edge may be gone.

How do you know the engines are independent?+

We measure it. The independence panel shows how often every pair of engines quotes the identical price. Rented skins sit near 100%; our five sit between 0.3% and 10.3%. When two brands share a desk, we count them once.

What does the confidence score mean?+

It is a transparency device, not a probability. It rises with the number of independent engines, a sharp stake limit behind the market, agreement between engines, and fresh prices, and every reason is shown so you can disagree with the inputs. It will be checked against closing-line results once they exist.

Can I cancel?+

Any time. Billing is monthly with no annual commitment, and cancelling stops the next charge.

how to read the numbers

Edge
· the price against the more conservative of two independent fair values, never the flattering one.
Leave-one-out consensus
· fair value from the other engines, excluding the book being quoted.
Pinnacle risks
· the stake a sharp book accepts on that market; a proxy for how firmly the price is held, not your book’s limit.
Quarter Kelly
· a quarter of the Kelly stake on a $5,000 bankroll, scaled down again by confidence.
