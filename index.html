35 sportsbooks don't mean 35 opinions

Ontario has 35 licensed sportsbooks. Most of them rent their odds from the same handful of trading desks — so a "consensus" built by averaging sportsbooks counts one desk's opinion six times. We measured which books are genuinely independent and built consensus from the engines.

How to read this. "Edge" is the gap between the price you can get and what the independent engines think the true probability is. "Pinnacle risks" is the stake the sharpest book in the market will accept — the best public proxy for how strongly a fair value is held. "Suggested stake" is quarter Kelly on the more conservative of our two fair values, scaled down again by our confidence in the row; it is deliberately smaller than the maths allows.

Odds move. Every figure is stamped with its build time. Nothing here is advice; bet responsibly, and only what you can afford to lose.
